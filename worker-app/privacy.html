Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

NEXVADORAQ HireHub helps workers discover job opportunities and companies publish vacancies and worker requests.

Information used

Depending on the features you use, the app may process your name, email address, mobile number, city, account role, profile photo, job applications, worker requests, and notifications.

Location

Worker location is optional. The app only requests GPS access after a worker chooses Start Live Location. Location sharing can be stopped using Stop Sharing. In this web preview, sharing works while the app is active and is not emergency or continuous background tracking.

How information is used

Information is used to create accounts, show relevant job and worker-request information, process applications, support hiring workflows, and deliver notifications.

Storage and service providers

Account and app data are stored using Firebase services configured for this project. Profile photos are stored in Firebase Storage. Do not upload Aadhaar numbers, bank details, passwords, or payment-card information.

Sharing

Information needed for a hiring interaction may be shown to the relevant worker or company. Location is shared with company accounts only while the worker has enabled location sharing. Do not share information you do not want a hiring contact to see.

Security and retention

Security rules are used to limit access to account and app records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Data may remain in backups or service logs for a limited period.

Your choices

You may stop location sharing, avoid uploading a profile photo, and stop using the service. For account or data deletion assistance, use the project support channel in the NEXVADORAQ GitHub repository and do not post personal information publicly.

Children

This service is intended for adults seeking work or hiring workers and is not designed for children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
